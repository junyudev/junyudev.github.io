Stable Signer

A message-conditioned watermarking framework for image provenance. The design balances model providers’ control over their models, a third party’s need for a shared extraction interface, and users’ exposure to quality loss and false attribution.

I contributed to the framework design, implementation, experiments, and analysis at Zhejiang University. The work is not yet published.
